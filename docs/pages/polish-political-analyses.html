Polish political analyses

Data and interactive charts on Polish politics, from my ongoing work on the party system, elections and public opinion. Each analysis is rebuilt from its data whenever the site is updated.

Polling tracker
A Bayesian poll-of-polls for the Sejm: voting intention since January 2025, seat projections for every week, polling-house effects, and a scenario builder for vote shares, joint lists and government options that keeps the model’s uncertainty.
Election results
Every presidential, Sejm, European Parliament and referendum vote since 1990: the facts and results of each, maps of every gmina and constituency, the change between any two elections, the partitions of 1914, each gmina’s history, and how the results line up with the make-up of the gminas.
Social and political trends
How Poles’ attitudes, values and lives have changed since 1990, each trend pooled from every comparable survey into one monthly estimate, with breakdowns by party preference, age, gender, education and place of residence. So far: support for membership of the European Union, and where Poles place themselves between left and right.
Party system indicators
Fragmentation, disproportionality, volatility, nationalisation, polarisation and the closure of government competition for every Sejm election since 1991 and for the current polls, with the lists, lineages, party positions and cabinets behind each number.
Party ideology
Where Polish parties stand on the economy, cultural questions, European integration and democracy, set against parties across Europe in the Chapel Hill Expert Survey since 2002, and how expert ratings of anti-elite rhetoric compare with The PopuList’s classification of parties as populist, far right or far left.
Polish parties in the Sejm, 1989–2026
The lifespans, Sejm seats, government status and genealogy of every party that has sat in the Sejm since 1989: the splits, mergers, renames and alliances that link them. Trace any party’s predecessors and successors.
