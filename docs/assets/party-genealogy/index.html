About the data
A defection is a move between clubs that the MP made: founding a splinter club, moving to another club, leaving a club to sit as unaffiliated, joining a club from unaffiliated status, or being expelled. Club renames, mergers and dissolutions are not counted. A move through unaffiliated status counts as two moves, unless the flows follow MPs through it.
Parties are the lead parties of the clubs on either side; a small club that no party leads is shown by its abbreviation. ≈ marks a date that is not documented: from 1997 the day of the MP's first roll-call vote in the new club, before 1997 a date imputed from the evidence, which the MP's record gives.
1997–2026 from Sejm API roll calls (the club at each vote); 1989–97 reconstructed from the Sejm archive and Wikipedia, with some intermediate moves missed.
